# Code Of Conduct

This project exists to help people build and maintain better software with AI coding agents. Contributions should make the repository easier to use, safer to maintain, and clearer for the next person.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique code, documentation, and decisions rather than people.
- Give enough context for others to reproduce, verify, or improve your suggestion.
- Assume public readers do not share your local setup or private history.
- Prefer clear, actionable feedback over vague approval or dismissal.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussion.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer direction about scope, safety, or disclosure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or lock discussions that violate this standard. Severe or repeated violations may result in blocking from the project.

For security-sensitive reports, use the private disclosure process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of public discussion.
